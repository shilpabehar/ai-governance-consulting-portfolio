# Stakeholder Interview 01 – AI Policy & Organizational Objectives

**Engagement:** Simulated AI Governance Assessment
**Assessment Area:** AI Policy & Organizational Context
**Frameworks:** ISO/IEC 42001, NIST AI RMF
**Stakeholder:** AI Governance / Management Representative
**Interview Status:** Completed
**Assessment Date:** October 08 2026

---

## 1. Objective

Validate the initial observation identified during the document review regarding the organization's AI policy and determine whether the organization has clearly defined AI objectives, leadership direction, and alignment between AI adoption and organizational priorities.

---

## 2. Initial Observation

During the document review, the AI policy was found to contain general principles relating to responsible AI use. However, the policy did not clearly demonstrate:

* The organization's overall objectives for AI.
* How AI supports organizational or business objectives.
* How AI priorities are established.
* How leadership provides direction for AI adoption.
* How the AI policy translates into operational decision-making.

**Initial assessment:** Potential gap requiring stakeholder validation.

---

## 3. Stakeholder Discussion

### Question 1 – Organizational AI Objectives

**What I asked:**

> Can you explain what the organization's overall objectives are for using AI, and how those objectives are reflected in the current AI policy?

**Stakeholder Response:**

> The organization is currently using AI primarily to improve employee productivity, automate repetitive activities, and support engineering teams. These objectives are understood by management, although they are not currently documented as formal AI objectives within the AI policy.

### My Assessment

The stakeholder confirmed that organizational objectives for AI exist in practice but are not explicitly documented within the AI governance framework.

**Assessment:** Partially established.

---

## 4. Follow-Up Investigation

### Question 2 – Leadership Involvement

**What I asked:**

> How were these AI objectives established, and which leadership or management roles were involved?

**Stakeholder Response:**

> AI priorities are discussed by senior management and individual business functions. However, there is currently no single documented approval mechanism or governance body responsible for establishing enterprise-level AI objectives.

### My Assessment

Leadership involvement exists, but the governance mechanism for establishing and formally approving AI objectives is not clearly defined.

**Assessment:** Potential governance gap.

---

## 5. Evidence Requested

The following evidence was requested to validate the stakeholder responses:

| Evidence                              | Status              | Assessment              |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------- |
| AI Policy                             | Available           | Reviewed                |
| AI Strategy / Roadmap                 | Not available       | Gap                     |
| Documented AI Objectives              | Not available       | Gap                     |
| Leadership AI Governance Records      | Partially available | Further review required |
| AI Initiative Prioritization Criteria | Not available       | Gap                     |

---

## 6. Validated Observation

The stakeholder discussion and evidence review confirmed that the organization has practical objectives for AI adoption; however, these objectives are not formally established within the AI governance framework.

Leadership discussions regarding AI exist, but there is no clear evidence of a defined mechanism for establishing, approving, and communicating enterprise-level AI objectives.

The original document-review observation is therefore **validated**.

---

## 7. Governance Impact

The absence of formally established AI objectives may make it difficult to demonstrate:

* Consistent prioritization of AI initiatives.
* Alignment between AI investments and organizational strategy.
* Clear leadership accountability for AI direction.
* Consistent assessment of whether proposed AI initiatives support organizational objectives.

---

## 8. Finding

**Finding ID:** GOV-01

**Title:** AI objectives are not formally established within the AI governance framework.

**Finding:**

> The organization has identified practical objectives for AI adoption, including productivity improvement and automation. However, these objectives are not formally documented within the AI governance framework, and a clearly defined mechanism for establishing, approving, and communicating enterprise-level AI objectives was not evidenced.

**Severity:** Medium

**Status:** Validated

---

## 9. Recommendation

Establish and formally document organizational AI objectives aligned with the organization's business strategy.

The organization should define:

1. Strategic objectives for AI adoption.
2. Leadership ownership and approval responsibilities.
3. Criteria for prioritizing AI initiatives.
4. Mechanisms for communicating AI objectives to relevant stakeholders.
5. Measures for evaluating whether AI initiatives continue to align with organizational objectives.

---

## 10. Required Follow-Up Evidence

To close the finding, the organization should provide evidence of:

* Approved AI objectives or strategy.
* Defined leadership accountability.
* AI initiative prioritization criteria.
* Communication of AI objectives to relevant stakeholders.
* Periodic review of AI objectives and alignment.
